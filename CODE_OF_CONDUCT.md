# Code of conduct

This project uses the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The full text there applies. This is a short form.

## Our pledge

We make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and respectful, also when you disagree.
- Give and accept useful feedback.
- Take responsibility for your mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

## Not acceptable

- Sexual language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing other people's private information without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that a reasonable person would think is not appropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when you represent the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainer, Meir Dick, at the email address on [github.com/meirdick](https://github.com/meirdick). Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer can remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code, and can warn, or temporarily or permanently ban, a contributor. The enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1 (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban) apply.
